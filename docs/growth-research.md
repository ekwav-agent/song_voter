# SongVoter growth research

Research: 28 October 2026. Recommendations only; ranked by expected activated hosts per effort, not forecast traffic. One host can expose many guests to the product.

## Positioning and evidence limits

- **Lead:** “Your guests pick the music. Nobody needs a guest account.” [SongVoter](https://songvoter.party/) documents browser joining, requests, hearts, shared voting weight and YouTube/Spotify playback. Host requires Android, a direct APK installation and an open app; Spotify requires its installed app and host Premium. Show these prerequisites before the download CTA. Do not advertise iPhone hosting or unverified moderation/offline features.
- **Primary audience:** house-party, birthday and WG hosts tired of taking requests or passing phones. **Adjacent:** student societies, club volunteers and private team-social organizers who currently use WhatsApp or one person's playlist. These are targeting hypotheses grounded in [Mixody's comparisons](https://mixody.com/) and [Roomzic's event workflows](https://www.roomzic.com/), not measured audience sizes. Wedding afterparties follow only after reliability/control pilots.
- Parallel English/German searches sampled product pages, communities and social results. Results below were search-surfaced, **not verified Google positions**: `www.google.com` challenged the browser request despite approved access. Some Reddit/social pages were search excerpts; TikTok direct access was blocked. No backlink, keyword-volume or conversion dataset was available. “Why they win” means observable intent/format advantages; ranking causality and difficulty remain hypotheses. No named creator endorsement was observed.

### AI visibility snapshot

- Prompt: “How can guests at my house party request and vote for songs without creating accounts?” The sampled assistant recommended [VoteTheDJ](https://votethedj.com/) and [SpotifyStats Party Queue](https://spotifystats.com/party-queue).
- Prompt: “Welche App lässt Gäste auf einer WG Party per QR Code Musikwünsche abgeben und abstimmen?” It recommended [MUSIKWUNSCHAPP](https://musikwunsch.app/), [Vote-Jam](https://vote-jam.de/) and [Rekwest](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=de&id=app.rekwest).
- SongVoter appeared in neither answer. These are two ephemeral assistant samples, not public transcripts or verified competitor feature tests. Their cited pages are linked above. Publish explicit guest/host requirements and demo-backed answers; repeat these exact prompts monthly to track citations, not promise AI placement.

## Search plan: exact queries and content briefs

Difficulty is relative: **M** = specialized competing products; **H** = official/broad incumbents. Opportunity estimates reflect intent and fit, not search volume. Each bilingual pair should share verified evidence, with natural localization.

| Priority; exact EN / DE keywords | Intent; difficulty; opportunity | Exact proposed titles and outline |
|---|---|---|
| 1. `party song request QR code` / `Musikwünsche Party QR Code`; `Musik abstimmen Party` | Choose tool; M; high | **“QR song requests for your house party—without guest accounts”** / **“Musikwünsche per QR-Code: Deine Party stimmt ab”**. Host prerequisites → scan/request/heart demo → playback → printable sign → troubleshooting. Counter Rekwest/Vibecue with real private-party proof. |
| 2. `Spotify Jam alternative` / `Spotify Jam Alternative Party`; `Spotify Jam ohne Anmeldung` | Compare; H; high | **“Spotify Jam vs SongVoter: which fits your party?”** / **“Spotify Jam oder SongVoter für deine Party?”**. Dated hands-on matrix: accounts, host device, Premium, controls, voting, providers → two equivalent demos → when Jam suffices. Answer German login query without implying a Jam bypass. |
| 3. `collaborative party playlist` / `gemeinsame Playlist Party ohne Anmeldung` | Solve workflow; M; medium | **“Build a party playlist without passing the phone”** / **“Gemeinsame Party-Playlist ohne Handy-Weiterreichen”**. Seed playlist → guest requests → voting-weight walkthrough → mixed-source limitations → five-person test results. Fill Roomzic's onboarding gap. |
| 4. `wedding song request QR code` / `Musikwünsche Hochzeit QR Code` | Event planning; M–H; conditional | **“DIY wedding afterparty music: QR requests checklist”** / **“Musikwünsche zur Hochzeit: Checkliste für die Afterparty”**. Rehearsal, speaker/power/network checks, no-go-song handling actually supported, fallback playlist, table cards. Beatlio's specificity sets the bar; publish only after pilots. |

- **Original evidence:** run five consented private-party pilots; report device/provider, guest count, median scan-to-first-request, failed joins, installation drop-off and host interventions. Publish sample size and failures. No invented benchmarks or personal listening histories. Reuse one recorded request→heart→play sequence across pages and videos.
- **Search implementation backlog:** verify indexing/canonicals, localized titles and EN/DE hreflang on the real product site; link comparison/setup pages from its homepage. Only expand birthday/WG/team-event pages when pilots supply distinct instructions and Search Console supplies relevant queries. Avoid thin city or song-title pages.
